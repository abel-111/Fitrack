🌿 5 Nutrition Rules for Real Fitness Results Practical Swaps ▾ Expand / Collapse

How to hit high protein, manage carbs, and retain authentic home flavor without extreme diets.

🍚 The 1-Cup Rice Rule

Limit cooked Ponni brown or Matta rice to 1 standard cup, and double your bowl of keerai kootu or vegetable poriyal for fiber.

🥘 Supercharge Sambar & Rasam

Add 2 boiled eggs, paneer cubes, or boiled soya chunks directly into hot vegetable sambar for an effortless 20-30g protein meal.

🌱 Sundal over Fried Bajjis

Make 1 cup of spiced kala chana, green peas, or sprouted moong sundal your regular 4 PM tiffin instead of deep-fried bajjis.

🥛 Neer Mor: Natural Electrolyte

Diluted buttermilk with crushed ginger, curry leaves, and a pinch of salt cools the gut, supports digestion, and restores minerals.

☕ Smart Filter Coffee

Enjoy your coffee guilt-free! Use toned milk and cut refined sugar. Having it 45 mins pre-workout gives natural focus and energy lift.
