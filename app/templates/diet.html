🌿 5 Nutrition Rules for Real Fitness Results

How to hit high protein, manage carbs, and retain authentic home flavor without extreme diets.

Practical Swaps
🍚 The 1-Cup Rice Rule

Never fear rice! Limit cooked Ponni brown or Kerala Red Matta rice to 1 standard cup, and double your bowl of keerai kootu or vegetable poriyal for fiber.

🥘 Supercharge Sambar & Rasam

Add 2 boiled eggs, pan-seared paneer cubes, or boiled soya chunks directly into your bowl of hot vegetable sambar for an effortless 20-30g protein meal.

🌱 Sundal over Fried Bajjis

Make 1 cup of spiced kala chana, green peas, or sprouted moong sundal your regular 4 PM tiffin instead of deep-fried bajjis or medu vadas.

🥛 Neer Mor: Natural Electrolyte

Diluted buttermilk with crushed ginger, curry leaves, and a pinch of pink salt cools the gut, supports digestion, and restores minerals after tough workouts.

☕ Smart Filter Coffee

Enjoy your degree coffee guilt-free! Use toned milk and cut down refined white sugar. Having it 45 mins pre-workout gives a wonderful natural energy lift.
